General Medicine

Our general medicine services cover annual wellness exams, chronic disease management, and acute illness treatment. CareShift partner physicians take time to understand your health history and coordinate referrals when specialized care is needed.

Patients can schedule same-week appointments for routine visits, lab work follow-ups, and medication reviews. Telehealth options are available for select consultations, making it easier to stay on top of your health between in-person visits.

  • Preventive screenings and wellness planning
  • Chronic condition monitoring and care plans
  • Coordinated referrals to specialist providers

Cardiology

Cardiology services include electrocardiograms, stress testing, and comprehensive heart health assessments. Our partner cardiologists work closely with primary care teams to manage hypertension, arrhythmias, and post-procedure follow-up care.

Early detection and ongoing monitoring are central to our cardiovascular program. Patients receive personalized treatment plans with clear guidance on lifestyle changes, medications, and when to seek urgent care.

Pediatrics

Pediatric care spans newborn check-ups, immunizations, developmental screenings, and adolescent health visits. CareShift partner clinics create welcoming environments where children and parents feel supported at every stage of growth.

Our pediatric teams emphasize preventive care and open communication with families. Same-day sick visits and extended hours help parents get timely answers when their child needs attention.

Orthopedics

Orthopedic services address joint pain, sports injuries, fractures, and mobility challenges. Partner specialists offer diagnostic imaging, physical therapy coordination, and surgical consultations when conservative treatment is not sufficient.

Recovery-focused care plans help patients return to daily activities safely. CareShift makes it easy to book follow-up visits and track rehabilitation progress through your patient portal.
